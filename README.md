# 🎯 Business Problem
Quick Commerce companies need to track not just sales, but also **delivery speed, rider performance, and dark store SLA compliance**. This dashboard helps to answer:
- Which city and category is driving sales?
- Are we breaching our SLA (Service Level Agreement)?
- Which vehicle type is more profitable and faster?
- What is the monthly sales and profit trend?

# 📌 Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** 778K
- **Total Profit:** 230K
- **Profit Margin:** 29.61%
- **Total Orders:** 3,375
- **Avg. Delivery Time:** 16.15 Mins
- **Avg. Rating:** 3.8 / 5

# 🎛️ Dashboard Features

**1. Dynamic Filters:**
- City (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad)
- Vehicle Type (EV Scooter, Petrol Bike)
- Month (January - December)

**2. Visual Breakdown:**

- **Total Sales by City:** Delhi leads with 39.48% (307K) of total sales, followed by Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Mumbai.
- **Total Sales by Category:** Munchies & Snacks (25.08%) and Fresh Produce (20.34%) are top categories.
- **Avg Delivery Time by Vehicle Type:** Petrol Bike is faster (15.1 Mins) compared to EV Scooter (16.8 Mins).
- **SLA Breach by Dark Store:** South Ext Hub has the highest SLA breaches (257), indicating operational bottlenecks.
- **Total Sales and Total Profit by Month:** Tracks seasonal trend and profitability month-on-month.
- **Total Profit and Total Sales by Vehicle Type:** Compares revenue vs profit for EV Scooter vs Petrol Bike.

# 💡 Key Insights

- **City Performance:** Delhi is the strongest market with ~39.5% sales share. Mumbai is lowest at 19.7%.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Petrol bikes deliver 1.7 mins faster than EV scooters on average, but EV scooters generate higher total sales.
- **SLA Bottleneck:** South Ext Hub and Hitex City Hub have the highest SLA breaches (>230), needs immediate process improvement and staff allocation.
- **Category Mix:** Munchies & Snacks and Fresh Produce contribute ~45% of sales, making them key for inventory planning.
